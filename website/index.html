Be yourself.
Keep your data.

Spoke is a Jolt app. It cannot run standalone. Install and start Jolt before opening Spoke. Then make a profile, share posts, reply in threads, and send private messages without creating an account that belongs to Spoke.

01 / Why Spoke

Social relationships
should survive software.

Your profile and connections should belong to you, not to whichever social app happens to be popular today.

  1. 01

    Bring your identity

    Spoke uses the identity already protected by Jolt. It does not create another username or ask for your private identity key.

  2. 02

    Approve the app

    You choose what Spoke may do. Jolt checks every request before publishing a post, reading your contacts, or opening a private message.

  3. 03

    Keep your connections

    If you replace Spoke with another compatible app, your identity and content do not need to disappear with it.

02 / What you can do

Share, reply,
and stay in touch.

Spoke gives you profiles, posts, conversations, and private messages, all tied to the identity you control through Jolt.

03 / Architecture

Spoke is the app.
Jolt is the home.

Spoke makes the social experience. It uses a scoped session with your local Jolt daemon, which holds your identity, checks every request, and joins the Jolt network.

IF SPOKE VANISHEDyour Jolt identity would still be yours.

WHAT SPOKE NEVER GETSyour private identity key.

Experimental v0

You can try it today.
Expect rough edges.

Spoke is desktop-first. Jolt must be running on your computer, and you approve Spoke before it can use your identity. It is not a finished public social network.
